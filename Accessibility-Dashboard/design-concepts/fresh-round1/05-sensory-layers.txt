Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: non-shipping design direction concept for a Korean web accessibility product landing page.

Materials/textures: Frosted acrylic, embossed paper, fine metal mesh, realistic edge highlights, shallow depth of field only in the lower visual zone.
Text (verbatim): Korean hero headline exactly “보이지 않던 장벽을, 이해할 수 있는 결과로.” Set once, perfectly legible, Korean-safe sans, weight 380, large display. Brand text exactly “UNI ACCESS”. CTA exactly “대시보드 열기”. Do not add any other readable text.

Constraints: No URL input. One CTA. Text zone must remain calm and high contrast. No functional text on transparent or busy material. No fake dashboard or numbers.
