Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: non-shipping design direction concept for a Korean web accessibility analytics landing page.
Primary request: Design a new civic-wayfinding landing page for UNI ACCESS, translating the logic of transit signage and public information systems into a trustworthy digital product page.
Scene/backdrop: Flat warm-gray page with a precise twelve-column grid and a continuous cobalt route line that enters from the top edge, turns at right angles through the hero, and continues into the next section.
Subject: Top-left compact wordmark, top-right single CTA, sparse navigation aligned to the route line. The hero headline is left-aligned and vertically centered, while the route line expands across the remaining canvas into four real HTML-like semantic zones represented by large labeled blocks and focus markers. The next section begins as a wide matrix whose row lines align perfectly with the hero route. No sealed cards.
Style/medium: Swiss public wayfinding, Korean civic design, and contemporary data editorial. Sharp rectangular geometry, tabular details, functional hierarchy, no decorative illustration.
Composition/framing: Straight-on full-page 3:2 landscape. Asymmetric 5/7 column distribution with extensive negative space. Headline and CTA above fold; matrix edge visible below.
Lighting/mood: Flat print-like color, confident, predictable, low-anxiety.
Color palette: pale concrete #E8E9E4, deep navy #101B35, cobalt #144FD1 as the only accent, white #FFFFFF. Do not use yellow, red, green, or purple.
Materials/textures: Smooth municipal enamel-sign finish, fine registration marks, hairline rules; essentially flat with no shadow.
Text (verbatim): Korean hero headline exactly “웹의 장벽을, 다음 작업으로 바꿉니다.” Set once, perfectly legible, Korean-safe sans, weight 650, upper-left, maximum two lines. Brand text exactly “UNI ACCESS”. CTA exactly “대시보드 열기”. Small category labels may be exactly “구조”, “인식”, “조작”, “이해”. No other readable text.
Dimensions: 1536x1024 landscape.
Constraints: No URL input. One CTA. Route line must visibly connect hero to next content. High contrast. Product identity understandable in five seconds. No three-card row.
Avoid: transit-map rainbow colors, playful icons, rounded rectangles, drop shadows, gradients, floating dashboard screenshots, centered headline, excessive micro-labels, fake scores, watermarks.
