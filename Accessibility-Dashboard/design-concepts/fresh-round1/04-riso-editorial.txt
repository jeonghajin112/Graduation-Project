Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: non-shipping visual direction concept for a Korean web accessibility product landing page.
Primary request: Design a new tactile risograph editorial landing page for UNI ACCESS that feels authored, investigative, and human without resembling a generic SaaS page.
Scene/backdrop: Warm white uncoated paper fills the page. Two oversized cropped risograph impressions—one black halftone browser-grid fragment and one vermilion correction mark—bleed off opposite edges and overlap with slight misregistration.
Subject: A small vertical wordmark and navigation rail sits on the far left. The hero headline begins one quarter into the page and is set in a refined Korean Myeongjo display face paired with sans-serif UI. One flat black CTA sits beneath it. A large diagonal proofing mark crosses the lower hero and reveals the next section, which resembles an edited manuscript with underlines and marginal explanations rather than cards.
Style/medium: Korean independent publication, risograph proof sheet, editorial design with disciplined digital interaction. Myeongjo display at medium weight, sans UI, generous paper space, irregular print crop.
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape full-page view. Asymmetric vertical rail plus open central reading column. Headline remains below 72px equivalent and no more than three lines. CTA is above fold. The bottom 18 percent previews the manuscript-like section.
Lighting/mood: Flat scanned-paper mood, thoughtful and quietly radical; no simulated spotlight.
Color palette: warm paper #F4F1E8, soft black #171713, vermilion #D94B2B as the only accent, graphite #69675F.
Materials/textures: Visible but restrained paper fibers, halftone dots, ink overlap, slight registration offset, sharp real type layer.
Text (verbatim): Korean hero headline exactly “누구도 놓치지 않는 웹을 만드는 일.” Set once, perfectly legible in a refined Korean Myeongjo display typeface, weight 500. Brand text exactly “UNI ACCESS”. CTA exactly “대시보드 열기”. No other readable text.
Dimensions: 1536x1024 landscape.
Constraints: No URL field. Only one CTA. The generated image is a concept mockup, but all text must look like a real layout layer, not printed onto an object. No fake stats or product dashboard.
Avoid: beige luxury styling, serif used for every element, nostalgic newspaper clichés, torn-paper collage overload, rounded cards, gradients, shadows, centered composition, cute icons, three equal cards, watermarks.
