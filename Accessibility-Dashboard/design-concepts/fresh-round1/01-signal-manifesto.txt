Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: non-shipping visual direction concept for a Korean web accessibility product landing page, desktop first viewport plus a visible hint of the next section.
Primary request: Design a completely new high-contrast “signal manifesto” landing page for UNI ACCESS, treating accessibility problems as clear actionable signals rather than scores.
Scene/backdrop: A flat carbon-black editorial canvas, not a browser screenshot floating in space. The page itself fills the image edge to edge.
Subject: A real landing-page composition. At top, a restrained wordmark “UNI ACCESS” on the left, three tiny navigation labels in the middle, and one sulfur-yellow action button on the right. The hero uses a strongly asymmetric grid: oversized Korean headline occupies the upper-left two thirds; the lower-right becomes a full-bleed field of crisp focus paths, underlines, cursor tracks, and numbered annotations made from real interface geometry. Below the fold, one continuous vertical repair path begins, not three feature cards.
Style/medium: Kinetic modernist poster design crossed with rigorous digital product art direction. Flat colors, sharp rules, overscale grotesque typography, one deliberate broken-grid gesture, and tactile 1% paper grain. No glossy 3D.
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape, straight-on full-page view, no device frame. Large quiet black zones create rhythm. The CTA remains visible above the fold. The hero visual is the stage, not a right-column card.
Lighting/mood: No simulated lighting. Calm urgency, high trust, visually assertive but not playful.
Color palette: carbon #10110F, warm white #F2F0E8, sulfur yellow #D8F04A as the only accent, muted graphite #73766E. The yellow occupies less than 10 percent of the page.
Materials/textures: Uncoated black paper texture at extremely low opacity, crisp vector rules, no blur.
Text (verbatim): Korean hero headline exactly “접근성은 점수가 아니라, 고칠 수 있는 일이어야 합니다.” Set once, perfectly legible, in a Korean-safe neo-grotesque sans, weight 650, upper-left, maximum three lines. Brand text exactly “UNI ACCESS”. CTA exactly “대시보드 열기”. Do not add any other readable body copy; use abstract short line marks for secondary content.
Dimensions: 1536x1024, landscape 3:2.
Constraints: No URL field. One primary CTA only. Navigation, headline, CTA, and next-section hint must all be visible. Strong WCAG contrast. No disability stereotypes. No fake statistics. No repeated cards.
Avoid: purple, blue gradients, neon glow, terminal hacker styling, rounded glass cards, centered hero, split-screen boxed dashboard, three equal feature cards, decorative blobs, emojis, logos other than the text wordmark, watermarks, browser chrome, mobile phone mockups.
